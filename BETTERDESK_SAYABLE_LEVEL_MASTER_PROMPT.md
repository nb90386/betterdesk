## Step 2 — choose a genuinely original visual system

**Proposed guiding concept: “From signal to certainty.”** A missed call or customer message enters a living signal field, resolves into one clear conversation, and lands as a confirmed appointment. An elegant visible journey from chaos to clarity. This is *a suggested starting direction, not a mandate*: if the website and Sayable research support a stronger unmistakably BetterDesk concept, articulate it and use it consistently.

Recommended art direction:

- **Signature mark:** design an original, simple BetterDesk `BD`/signal motif usable at 24px and as a large spatial element. Export real SVG/favicon/OG treatments. It must not resemble a stock AI sparkle logo.
- **Palette:** restrained ink/graphite, rich near-black, tactile pearl/ivory, brushed silver and a single warm amber/gold light source. Build coherent dark-to-light pacing, not an all-black void or blue/purple template. Exact colors and type choices must arise from design studies; document rationale and accessibility.
- **Materials:** dimensional glass and metal only where they clarify the physical product presentation. Layer true dark surfaces, subtle grain, edge reflections, believable soft light, ambient occlusion, shadow falloff, and shallow depth-of-field in a performant way. No ubiquitous glow blobs, random gradients, fake specular overload or uncontrolled visual noise.
- **Typography:** one standout high-craft display face paired with an extremely readable text/UI face; choose legitimate licensed or open fonts, refine line breaks, tracking, optical sizes, responsive sizes, and rhythm. Set typography as design system tokens.
- **Composition:** full-bleed product stage, crop/overlap/scale and editorial asymmetric grids. Don't use a standard centered headline + three cards, or a formulaic 50/50 hero. Avoid big empty spaces. Every section must earn its viewport height. Hero, transitions, and typography must look art-directed on both desktop and phone.
- **Products, not abstract hype:** create bespoke product UI assets with coherent sample names, timestamps, message threads, qualification steps, available slots and staff controls. Every UI visual should feel plausible. Label simulated walkthroughs. Don't make a fake CRM admin console the main sales story.
- **Personality:** understated confidence, human service-business language. No “revolutionize,” “seamlessly unlock,” em dashes, fake social proof, invented integration logos or unverifiable KPIs.

Create `docs/quality-v2/ART_DIRECTION.md` containing: design tokens, font choices, light/shadow language, surface hierarchy, brand shape vocabulary, photographic/graphic direction, page rhythm, moodboard/reference screenshots when available, and a 12-line creative rationale. No implementation until this is concrete; then proceed quickly.

## Step 3 — make four memorable, useful set pieces

These are mandatory areas of investment, not a request to sprinkle effects everywhere:

**A. Cinematic hero / the money moment.** Use an unmistakable original composition with a large elegant headline readable in one glance, direct human subcopy, one primary demo CTA and one secondary product link. Under/around it a *physically convincing, product-specific* animated scene: missed-call signal arrives; a reply is formed; conversation resolves; a booked appointment settles into the schedule. Include hand-built vector line work, controlled particles, sophisticated lighting, spatial layering, clipping, shared elements, and refined perspective, choosing only effects that improve the scene. The first meaningful frame must be impressive before animation loads. Do not require cursor movement to understand it. No forced autoplay with audio. Mobile needs a different composition, not desktop squeezed smaller.

**B. Scrollcraft product story.** 3–5 states in a carefully choreographed scroll-driven section: inquiry, response, qualification, handoff/booking, owner visibility. One cohesive visual persists across states and transforms naturally using masks, crossfades, curves/arcs, shared-layout movement, focus lighting and deliberate camera framing. Include semantic content outside the animation and a real reduced-motion/touch fallback. Avoid horizontal scroll hijacking, needless pinning, or long stretches with nothing happening.

**C. Realistic interactive demo.** The current scenario selector/product explorer must feel like real software: believable consistent data and proper states, precise UI details, smooth selection/expansion, clear role of human staff, keyboard interaction and accessibility. Do not rebuild a nonfunctional fake. Distinguish simulated from live features. If no real AI endpoint is configured, use transparent illustrative sample data.

**D. Revenue / ROI and conversion scene.** The savings or revenue-at-risk calculator should have an editorial, premium interactive visual that *teaches* the impact of missed inquiries, with transparent assumptions, adjustable inputs, credible number animations and correct units. Do not use fictional revenue recovery claims. Follow with a memorable, tasteful CTA composition, not a loud off-brand glowing button.

Across the site, add 1–2 secondary distinctive moments (e.g., a restrained animated product network or physically lit product detail), but **do not add effects just to count them**. Prioritize the quality of 4 highlights over 40 generic animations.
