# Founder visual review checklist — BetterDesk v2

Inspect the most recent local screenshots and the actual preview; don't rate the old production alias.

- [ ] Hero is immediately recognizable and clearly about BetterDesk, not a generic AI startup.
- [ ] Unique original brand mark, designed graphics, type, lighting and material treatment.
- [ ] Headline says what the business accomplishes in five seconds; copy is human, concise, no fake proof.
- [ ] Large-scale visual shows a missed inquiry becoming a booked appointment.
- [ ] Signature scroll choreography clarifies workflow; feels natural on mouse, trackpad and touch.
- [ ] Interactive sample UI is beautiful, believable, usable, and labeled as illustrative where appropriate.
- [ ] Every section has different intentional art direction without losing one coherent brand.
- [ ] Desktop screenshots look composed at 1440 and 1920, mobile at 320 and 390; no cutoff or unearned blank space.
- [ ] Pricing is clear, accurate, aligned; CTAs work; actual contact route works or accurately explains fallback.
- [ ] 3 full screenshot-to-critique-to-fix loops with before/after evidence, not only test reports.
- [ ] Reduced motion, keyboard, screen readers, Safari/WebKit, build and mobile Lighthouse checked honestly.
- [ ] Latest preview URL actually loads; correct Vercel project, no unapproved production change.

The founder decides whether the final design has Sayable-level quality. Tests and self-scored ratings alone cannot establish this.
