# Logo decision

**Chosen: L1 "door swing b".** A wall (stem) and an open door's swing (quarter disc), the symbol on every floor plan a cleaning contractor brings to a walkthrough, set so it reads as a lowercase b. The gap between wall and swing is the opening.

- Geometry (32 grid): wall `rect x=6 y=3 w=5 h=26 rx=1.2`; swing `M13.5 29V13.5A15.5 15.5 0 0 1 29 29z`.
- Wordmark: "BetterDesk" set live in General Sans 620, −0.032em, mark at 1.2em with a −0.04em optical drop.
- Favicon / app icon: the mark knocked into a graphite tile (`app/icon.svg`, `app/apple-icon.tsx`). Mono and light versions in `public/brand/`.
- Explored: 6 symbols + 2 wordmarks, rendered at 16/24/32/64/128 px on light and dark (`docs/reinvention/logo/board.html` → `screenshots/logo-board-r1.png`). Rejected: L3 double-acting door (busy pinwheel), L4 interrupted D (generic), L5 directory lines (reads as a text-align icon), L6 threshold (unclear), W2 lowercase with mark-as-b (reads "letterdesk").
- Replaced everywhere: header, footer, app sidebar, favicon, apple icon, OG image, JSON-LD logo. V2–V4 marks removed from `public/`.
- **Trademark clearance: NOT performed.** No claim of exclusive rights.
- **Open risk (from the jury critic):** at 20 px in the header the mark can read as a small bar chart rather than a door. Options for the founder: enlarge the swing relative to the wall, or draw the swing as an arc + door leaf (truer to plan notation, lighter at small sizes).
