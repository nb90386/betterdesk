Frequently asked
Real answers to real questions.
What does BetterDesk actually do?
BetterDesk is an AI-powered front desk for service businesses. It answers inbound calls, texts, web forms, DMs, and emails in seconds, in your business's voice. It qualifies leads, books appointments, runs follow-up sequences, requests reviews, and surfaces the conversations and revenue your business would otherwise miss.
Does BetterDesk replace my receptionist?
No. BetterDesk works alongside the people you already have. It handles the overflow, the calls when your team is busy, the texts after hours, the web forms at 11pm. Your team focuses on customers in front of them; BetterDesk handles the rest.
Can I approve responses before they go out?
Yes. You decide. Autonomous mode (it replies on its own), assisted mode (it drafts, you approve), or escalation mode (it handles routine, you handle sensitive). Most accounts start in assisted for the first week, then graduate to autonomous as trust builds.
Does it work after hours and on weekends?
Yes, that's one of the biggest reasons businesses adopt it. BetterDesk replies 24/7. You can configure quiet hours, emergency routing, and what gets booked vs. queued for the next morning.
Can it book real appointments on a real calendar?
Yes. BetterDesk integrates with the calendar and scheduling tools you already use, Google Calendar, popular practice-management systems, and major field-service platforms. It checks availability live and books on the spot, with confirmations and reminders included.
What businesses is it best for?
Local service businesses with high-intent inbound: dentists, med spas, HVAC, plumbers, contractors, salons, auto shops, cleaning companies, fitness studios, and small agencies. If a missed call costs you a real customer, BetterDesk is built for you.
How does it improve over time?
BetterDesk learns from your approved business data, real customer language, booking patterns, and your edits. It improves suggestions, follow-up timing, and FAQs week over week. It does not invent claims about your business, and you can override or correct any pattern.
How fast can I launch?
Most businesses are live in under 48 hours. Onboarding is a guided session, we calibrate BetterDesk to your services, hours, pricing, and tone, connect your phone and inbox, and run test conversations before you flip the switch.
How much revenue can it recover?
It depends on your job value, missed-lead volume, and current close rate. Use the calculator above to model it for your business. Most service businesses see BetterDesk pay for itself within 1–3 recovered appointments per month.
Is it worth the monthly cost?
For most service businesses, one recovered job per month covers a big share of the cost. After that, every recovered conversation is net new revenue you weren't going to earn otherwise. Cancel any time if it doesn't deliver.
