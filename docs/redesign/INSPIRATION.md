# Inspiration → principles (not copies)

Research method tonight: prior first-hand familiarity with these public sites plus the founder's 100-point checklist. Live screenshot study of attio.com / linear.app / apple.com was deprioritized to protect build time; add it in a later pass if useful.

| # | Source | Pattern | Takeaway for BetterDesk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attio | Product illustrations built from real UI fragments, cropped tightly, with labeled callouts | Hero scene is composed from real-looking BetterDesk UI fragments (call card, SMS thread, calendar slot, recap) tied together by the thread, not one floating phone. |
| 2 | Attio | Editorial section headers with small mono eyebrow + large serif statement, left-aligned | Left-aligned editorial headings; mono eyebrows numbered like ledger entries. |
| 3 | Attio | Navigation discipline: few top-level items, quiet bar | Five items + one CTA. No mega-menu until there is enough content to justify one. |
| 4 | Linear | Typographic precision, tight tracking on display, generous line-height on body | Display tracking −0.02em, body 1.6, measure ≤ 62ch. |
| 5 | Linear | Product detail density next to spacious statements | Alternate a spacious statement band with a dense explorer panel. |
| 6 | Linear | Motion is short and purposeful (fade + 4–8px translate) | 180–240ms ease-out entrances, 30–40ms stagger, no bounce. |
| 7 | Apple | One idea per viewport, confident pacing | Each homepage band makes one point; the headline carries it alone. |
| 8 | Apple | Light and contact shadow to make objects feel physical | Appointment cards and phone fragments get soft contact shadows and a 1px top highlight, like card stock on a desk. |
| 9 | Stripe (checklist) | Diagrams that explain a flow better than prose | The through-line diagram explains capture → reply → book → follow up → recap. |
| 10 | Ramp (checklist) | Benefit-first copy, plain numbers, low-friction CTA | Plain-language headings; the only numbers are prices and user inputs. |

## Do not copy
- Attio's colour system, glyph style, or its specific product-card layouts.
- Linear's dark gradient hero, glow lines, or its logo grid.
- Apple's product photography language or SF typography.
- Any HTML/CSS/assets from these sites.

## BetterDesk response
Paper-and-ink editorial shell, a single vermilion "unanswered" signal that resolves to desk-green "handled", and the through-line motif carrying a customer from first ring to the owner's recap.
