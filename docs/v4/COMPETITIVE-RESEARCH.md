# BetterDesk V4 — Competitive Research

All pages checked **2026-11-07** via WebFetch of the official URL shown. Pages were read through an automated text extractor, so plan-table checkmarks can be lost. **Evidence** = stated on that page. **Assumption** = researcher inference. "Not observed" means the fetched page did not show it, **not** that the product lacks it. Prices are USD list prices and change often; re-check before quoting.

## Horizontal sales, CRM and prospecting

### GoHighLevel — https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing
- **Evidence:** Starter $97/mo, Unlimited $297/mo, top plan $497/mo (named "Founder" on the plan card, "Pro" in the FAQ, so the naming is inconsistent). Unlimited contacts and users on all plans; Starter has 3 sub-accounts (FAQ). Core features: CRM & Pipelines, Unified Conversations, Booking Calendars, Workflow Automation, email/SMS (usage-billed), Conversation AI / Voice AI / Reviews AI (usage-billed), Prospecting tool. Add-ons: AI Employee $50 or $97/mo per sub-account; Premium prospecting $29/mo per sub-account. Upper plans can rebill usage with markup.
- **Not observed:** proposals/estimates; a vertical template for janitorial.
- **Assumption:** sold mostly through agencies; a contractor buying direct faces a large setup burden.

### Apollo — https://www.apollo.io/pricing · https://docs.apollo.io/docs/api-pricing
- **Evidence:** plans Free, Basic, Professional, Custom; **dollar prices were not shown in the fetched page text**. Credits are pooled, reset each cycle, don't roll over, and cover emails, phones, warmup, mailboxes and AI-researched data. Sequencing is on Basic and above. API: people enrichment 1 credit (+8 if a mobile is returned); org enrichment 1 credit; org search 1 credit per page of up to 100; waterfall email 1–4 (can exceed 20) and phone 8–25 credits.
- **Not observed:** API access by plan; per-credit dollar price; data-retention terms on the API page.
- **Assumption:** strongest on office-worker contacts; weaker coverage of facility managers at small local businesses.

### Clay — https://www.clay.com/pricing
- **Evidence:** Free (500 actions, 100 data credits/mo); Launch $167/mo headline; Growth $446/mo headline. The FAQ on the same page says $185 / $495, so the page contradicts itself. 150+ enrichment providers, waterfalls on all plans, Claygent AI web-research agent on all plans, BYO API keys. CRM sync, HTTP API and webhooks from Growth. Data credits from $0.05. No charge when enrichment returns nothing.
- **Assumption:** built for growth/RevOps operators; too complex for a cleaning-company owner. Could become a data source BetterDesk integrates with.

### HubSpot Sales Hub — https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales

The page doesn't label which price is monthly and which annual. HubSpot Credits: 500 / 3,000 / 5,000 per month on Starter, Pro and Enterprise, $0.010 each, no rollover, used by AI agents. Prospecting Agent listed under Agent Hub (tier unclear). Meeting links, sequences (Starter: 500 sends/user/day), pipelines (15 on Starter).

### Close — https://www.close.com/pricing
- **Evidence:** Solo, Essentials, Growth and Scale at $19/$9, $49/$35, $109/$99 and $149/$139 per seat (the page shows two prices without labelling monthly vs annual). "Chloe" AI on every plan, 500–2,000 AI credits/user/mo. Calling (~$0.02/min usage), lead enrichment on all plans, calendar sync, notetaker. Automated email outreach and bulk email from Growth.

## Synthesis

### Where BetterDesk should integrate, not compete
- **Operations** (scheduling, inspections, time clock, payroll, work orders): Swept, OrangeQC, Janitorial Manager, CleanGuru, Jobber. Hand off a won contract by export or webhook; never rebuild ops.
- **Data enrichment:** Apollo, Clay and Google Places as optional connectors behind customer-owned keys or credits. BetterDesk should not become a data broker.
- **Mailbox and calendar:** Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 for sending approved introductions and booking walkthroughs. Resend's AUP forbids cold outreach and Postmark's terms require permission-based lists (both verified), so transactional ESPs are for notifications only.
- **Accounting and e-sign:** QuickBooks and existing e-sign tools, later.
- **Systems of record:** for customers already on HubSpot or Jobber, sync rather than replace (assumption: a later phase).

### What BetterDesk uniquely simplifies (claims to prove, not yet proven)
1. **Vertical defaults:** pipeline stages that end in a *site walkthrough* and a *recurring contract*, not a generic "deal". None of the horizontal tools above showed janitorial-specific stages (not observed).
2. **Explainable qualification:** each fit score shows its evidence (source, date, rule). Clay and Apollo offer AI research, but aimed at RevOps users. BetterDesk's bet is that an owner can use the same idea in minutes.
3. **Approval-first outreach by default:** sending off, human approval required. This is a trust and compliance stance, not a feature others can't copy.
4. **Inbound and outbound in one board:** commercial inquiries triaged away from residential and job-seeker noise, landing in the same pipeline as Scout's prospects.

### What cannot yet be defended as differentiation
- **"AI-powered"**: GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Attio, Close, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan and Podium all market AI (evidence above).
- **Pipeline/CRM, unified inbox, booking calendars**: commodity features (GoHighLevel core, Jobber Pipeline add-on $49, HubSpot Free).
- **Lead data quality**: BetterDesk has no proprietary data, and Google Places storage limits restrict building one (see pricing doc).
- **Price**: GoHighLevel Starter ($97), CleanGuru ($79–$159) and Jobber Core ($29–$49) bracket the $99 entry tier.
- **Industry fit**: unproven until interviews confirm owners want a sales tool separate from ops. Aspire and CleanGuru already combine CRM and bidding for this buyer.
- **No customers, case studies or outcome metrics exist.** No differentiation claim should appear in marketing until a pilot produces evidence.
