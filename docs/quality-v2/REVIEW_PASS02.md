# Pass-02 Design Review (screenshots only)

## 1. Verdict
**REJECT** - the dark restyle is a real step up from the baseline, but the page still has dead voids, empty/invisible mid-state frames, a copy-and-UI-heavy hero with no single focal moment, and subpages that are the same template with a dark banner stapled on.

## 2. More crafted than baseline? Yes (but not yet Sayable-level)
- Baseline home-1440-top: beige serif hero, a floating missed-call card connected by a hand-drawn squiggle to a small phone, with the whole right half below y=520 empty. It reads as a template.
- Pass-02 home-1440-top: a Bricolage-style tight-tracked two-tone headline, a believable chat thread, a schedule rail with a "Holding for Dana M." slot, an amber accent and a coherent dark world. The product is demonstrated (call -> text -> calendar hold), not just described.
- It is still not at the "obviously expensive" bar. The craft is concentrated in the hero and the type; the rest of the page rhythm is uneven (see flaws).

## 3. Ten hard flaws, ranked
1. **Dead dark voids in "Follow one call to calendar"** (home-1440-full ~y 1450-3900 of 9508; home-1440-mid left half). The pinned-scroll step list is 4 dim, near-invisible steps (contrast far below AA) with 300-400px of nothing between them, and the left column of home-1440-mid is an empty black field with a lone amber tick. Fix: keep the active step at full contrast, shrink inactive steps to ~60% opacity (not 15%), cut the step height to ~60vh, and fill the left rail with a persistent timestamp/clock so it reads as a timeline, not a void.
2. **Hero has no focal hierarchy** (home-1440-top). The headline (two lines, 90px) is dominant, but the three product fragments (missed-call node, chat, schedule) are all similar size/weight with equal-opacity grey text; nothing says "look here". The giant 8:00 dashed slot is a tall empty rectangle (~180px of nothing). Fix: make the chat phone 15-20% larger and elevated (lit rim, stronger shadow), shrink the schedule to a compact rail, and shorten the 8:00 slot to ~64px with a mini booking-confirmation inside it.
3. **Product UI empty in captured states** (home-1440-mid2: the walkthrough phone is a blank cream rectangle; reply-mode card shows near-invisible tab labels "Assisted/Escalation/Autonomous" and an invisible draft bubble). If these frames are mid-animation they still ship a state that looks broken to anyone who scrolls fast. Fix: render the final state server-side/by default and animate *to* it; never begin at opacity 0 for content inside the viewport at entry; add `prefers-reduced-motion` static state.
4. **Dark-to-cream seams are abrupt and mismatched** (home-1440-mid2 at y~380: hard cut from cream walkthrough to dark reply-mode; home-1440-full ~y 4400 gradient then cream). The sequence dark / cream / dark / cream / cream feels random. Fix: pick a rule (dark for narrative, light only for the interactive walkthrough and pricing), use one feathered transition, and drop the cream footer-adjacent CTA band so the close matches the hero.
5. **Subpages still look like the old design with a dark banner** (pricing-1440-top, platform-1440-top, demo-1440-top). Hero text left, right 50% of the banner empty; beneath is the old cream template (white cards, mono "Best for:" captions, green pills). No product art in any subpage hero. Fix: put one hero artifact on each (pricing: price-per-recovered-job dial; platform: the inbox peeking up into the banner; walkthrough: the phone), and carry dark-mode material (hairline rims, amber accent) into the cream cards.
6. **Walkthrough toolbar clipping** (demo-1440-top y~612: "Late-night quote request CLEANI..." is truncated; the "Illustration - sample data" label collides with the Without/With toggle). Fix: make the scenario tabs a scrollable/segmented control with a fade mask or shrink labels, and move the sample-data note to the card footer.
7. **Mobile hero stack is long and CTA split** (home-390-top, home-320-top). The hero text plus two CTAs is fine, but "Watch a sample call" drops to its own line at 320 (left-aligned, orphaned from the primary), and the missed-call node is oversize with the phone number wrapping to "(512) 555-0148 / . 6:42 PM" with a stray middle dot at line start. Fix: stack CTAs full-width consistently, remove the dot on wrap (use separate lines), and cap the node at 56px.
8. **Mobile sticky CTA overlaps content** (home-390-mid, bottom ~y 1550). A cream bar ("Plans from $149/mo. Setup with a person." + Book a demo) sits on the dark page with content bleeding through it (the card text is visible under the bar), and its cream color breaks the dark world. Fix: opaque dark bar with a hairline top border and a 12px safe-area gutter; shorten the copy to the price alone.
9. **Page rhythm: too many equal-weight sections** (home-1440-full). After the hero, "phone rings" (three columns), "follow one call", "same call two endings", "you decide", "built for", "pricing", FAQ, CTA are all heading-left/artifact-right with similar scale. No peak moments, no scale break, no full-bleed statement. Fix: promote one section (the "two endings" comparison) to a full-bleed split with oversized numerals, and merge "built for businesses" into a compact strip.
10. **Typography details** (home-1440-top, bottom). The second headline line in grey at ~60% reads as disabled rather than quiet and fails contrast in places (check #9a9aa6 on near-black for 90px is fine, but the small grey microcopy under the CTA at 13px ~#777 is below 4.5:1). Also the CTA band headline "Wake up to / bookings, not / voicemails." breaks into three short lines leaving a hollow right-hand column on cream, and the tilted "Dana M. at 8:00" card is rotated, so its text is anti-aliased/blurry. Fix: raise microcopy to >=#a0a0a8, set the CTA headline to two lines at max-width ~14ch, remove card rotation or use rotate(-1deg) with will-change/3D transforms off.

## 4. Scores (1-10)
- **Originality: 6** - the Bricolage two-tone head + amber thread is distinctive, but the layout is still hero / three columns / sticky story / pricing.
- **Hero impact: 6** - strong type, but the product trio lacks a focal point and has an empty schedule slot.
- **Art direction: 6** - coherent dark+amber, but the cream islands and the old subpages break the world.
- **Product demonstration: 7** - believable chat, calendar hold and rule card, and the walkthrough exists; many frames show blank states.
- **Motion (inferred): 5** - mid-flight frames show invisible text and blank phone screens, so entrance choreography leaves unreadable in-view content.
- **Typography: 7** - tight headline, readable body, good mono usage; small-text contrast and line breaks need work.
- **Information clarity: 7** - what/for whom/price are clear in the hero; the middle of the page is slow.
- **Page rhythm: 4** - voids in the story section, random dark/cream alternation, no peak moments.
- **Business copy: 7** - concrete (Ridgeline, 78704, Thursday 8-10) and honest ("Sample data"); somewhat long in places.
- **Mobile execution: 5** - no horizontal clipping seen, but orphan CTA at 320, the sticky bar overlapping, and the 27,810px page height is far too long.
- **Conversion/trust: 6** - price visible and a clear primary CTA, but there are no real proof points (customers, numbers, security/consent statement), and the sample-data caveats outweigh trust cues.
